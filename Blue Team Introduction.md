Your Daily Duties<br>
Reviewing security alerts as they come in<br>
Sitting in on team discussions about ongoing issues<br>
Working with other teams when something affects them too<br>
Keeping up with new attack techniques, since they change often

Investigate the attack You'll need to...
1. Find the important alert and copy the IP address.<br>
2. Investigate the IP address by using the "IP Hunter".<br>
3. Escalate the alert to the correct person.<br>
4. Block the IP address from trying again while we investigate

Security Hierarchy<br>
Cyber security priorities are different for every company.

<img src="https://i.imgur.com/c2h01ER.png" height="60%" width="60%">

Red Team: Offensive security experts, pentesters, or ethical hackers who look for security issues<br>
GRC Team: Specialists managing policies and ensuring compliance with regulations like PCI DSS<br>
Blue Team: Defensive security experts like SOC analysts, engineers, or incident responders

<img src="https://i.imgur.com/9C3OOY7.png" height="60%" width="60%">

<img src="https://i.imgur.com/J7XUL8R.png" height="60%" width="60%">

Digital Forensics Analyst: Uncover hidden threats in disk and memory<br>
Threat Intelligence Analyst: Gather data about emerging threat groups<br>
AppSec Engineer: Maintain a secure software development lifecycle<br>
AI Researcher: Study AI threats and how to defend against them
