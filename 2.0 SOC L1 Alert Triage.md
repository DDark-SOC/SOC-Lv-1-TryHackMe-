L1 Role in Alert Triage<br>
SOC L1 analysts are the first line of defence, and they are the ones who work with alerts the most. Depending on various factors, L1 analysts may receive zero to a hundred alerts a day, every one of which can reveal a cyberattack. Still, everyone in the SOC team is somehow involved in the alert triage:<br>
SOC L1 analysts:  Review the alerts, distinguish bad from good, and notify L2 analysts in case of a real threat<br>
SOC L2 analysts:  Receive the alerts escalated by L1 analysts and perform deeper analysis and remediation<br>
SOC engineers:  Ensure the alerts contain enough information required for efficient alert triage<br>
SOC manager:  Track speed and quality of alert triage to ensure that real attacks won't be missed

Alert Prioritisation<br>

1-Filter the alerts:<br>
Make sure you don't take the alert that other analysts have already reviewed, or that is already being investigated by one of your teammates. You should only take new, yet unseen and unresolved alerts.<br>
2-Sort by severity:<br>
Start with critical alerts, then high, medium, and finally low. This is because detection engineers design rules so that critical alerts are much more likely to be real, major threats and cause much more impact than medium or low ones.<br>
3-Sort by time:<br>
Start with the oldest alerts and end with the newest ones. The idea is that if both alerts are about two breaches, the hacker from the older breach is likely already dumping your data, while the "newcomer" has just started the discovery.
